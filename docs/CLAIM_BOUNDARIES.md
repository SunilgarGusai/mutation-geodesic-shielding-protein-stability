# Scientific claim boundaries

## Supported

- MGSC provides an exact fixed-topology decomposition of off-mutation shortest-path distances into mutation-bypassing and mutation-containing routes under the study's graph model.
- Exhaustive target-free verification over 382,543 development mutations found exact agreement between MGSC-certified whole-mutation shielding and directly computed geodesic silence.
- The independent external benchmark supports an association between greater site-level geodesic susceptibility and more destabilizing mutation landscapes within proteins.
- A same-site analysis supports an opposite substitution-level contrast: among substitutions at the same site, propagation-positive substitutions have higher / less destabilizing experimental ΔΔG on average than shielded substitutions.

## Not supported

- MGSC is not a thermodynamic energy.
- MGSC does not establish physical allostery or causal signal propagation.
- The primary nonlinear added-value analysis did not establish robust ΔΔG prediction improvement beyond the direct structural/chemical baseline.
- The external site-level association did not remain supported as independent after adjustment for rSASA, packing/contact count, local MJ96 interaction sum, and mean absolute direct perturbation.
- The explicit-mutant structural comparison is descriptive rather than confirmatory because only 5 mixed structural clusters were available versus the prespecified minimum of 8.

These boundaries are part of the scientific result and must not be weakened in downstream reuse.
