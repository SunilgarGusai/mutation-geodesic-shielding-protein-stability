# Literature update through 2026

The final manuscript literature pass includes current work on protein stability prediction, local mutation representations, residue-interaction networks, and graph-based mutation analysis.

Representative recent methods discussed in the manuscript include PROST (2022), ProSTAGE (2024), GeoStab-suite (2024), Stability Oracle (2024), DDGemb (2025), UniMutStab/shared-weight graph models (2025), and JanusDDG (2026), alongside classical structure/physics baselines and the Tsuboyama mega-scale stability resource.

The novelty boundary is intentionally narrow. The study does not claim novelty for residue-interaction networks, shortest paths, replacement paths, statistical residue-pair potentials, graph neural networks, mutation propagation, or protein-stability prediction itself. The contribution is the specific mutation-deleted bypass versus mutation-containing route shielding formulation, its exact fixed-topology interpretation, exhaustive target-free characterization, independent stability associations, and transparent negative/inconclusive boundaries.
